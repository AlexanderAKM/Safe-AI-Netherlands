The Netherlands' national AI Safety initiative

Building a safer future with AI

Safe AI Netherlands (SAIN) unites researchers, students, and professionals across the country to ensure artificial intelligence is developed and deployed responsibly.

Explore Our Research Get Involved
What We Do
Four pillars of impact

Research

Connecting talented researchers with PhD+ supervisors for impactful AI Safety projects.

Education

Running "AI Safety, Ethics, and Society" courses in Technical and Governance tracks.

Events

Hackathons, AI Safety Chats, and expert seminars bringing the community together.

Outreach

Raising awareness about AI Safety through Substack, social media, and partnerships.
