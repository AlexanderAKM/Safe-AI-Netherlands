What We Do
Four pillars of impact
01

Research

Our national Research Hub connects talented researchers with PhD+ supervisors for impactful AI Safety projects.
02

Education

We run the "AI Safety, Ethics, and Society" course in Technical and Governance tracks across our local chapters.
03

Events

From technical hackathons to AI Safety Chats and expert seminars, we host regular events for the community.
04

Outreach

Through our Substack, social media, and partnerships, we raise awareness about AI Safety across the Netherlands.
