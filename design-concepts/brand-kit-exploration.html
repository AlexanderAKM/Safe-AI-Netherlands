01 — Naming
SAIN vs SAINE
Both names sound identical when spoken aloud ("sane") — perfect for an AI safety org. Here's how they stack up.
SAIN
Safe AI Netherlands

Strengths

  • Short, punchy, four letters
  • Clean acronym, immediately parseable
  • Already in use — existing equity with team and early community
  • "SAIN Groningen" chapter naming works well

Risks

  • "SAIN - Singular AI Network" is a direct competitor in AI space
  • Multiple AI-adjacent "Sain" brands (Sainapse, Sainika)
  • Harder to dominate search results
  • Limited expansion beyond Netherlands in name
SAINE
Safe AI Netherlands • Europe

Strengths

  • Zero conflicts in the AI / tech space
  • Same "sane" pronunciation — perfect connotation
  • The E creates expansion room (Europe, Education, Everywhere)
  • More distinctive in search, easier to own SEO
  • Sounds like French "saine" = healthy/sound — fits mission
  • "SAINE Groningen" chapter naming still works

Risks

  • Existing brand equity with SAIN needs migration
  • Could look like a typo of "SAIN" at first glance
  • Five letters slightly less punchy than four
