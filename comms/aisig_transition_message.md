# **A message to the AISIG, now SAIN Groningen, community**

Hey everyone,

You might have noticed some changes coming, or maybe this is the first you're hearing about it. Either way, here's the short version:

**AISIG is becoming [SAIN Groningen](https://safeainetherlands.org/chapters/groningen)**, a local chapter of [**Safe AI Netherlands (SAIN)**](https://safeainetherlands.org/), a new national AI safety organization.

## **Why?**

Over the past years, AISIG has grown into something much bigger than a student group in Groningen. We've published at NeurIPS and ICLR. We've run 9+ course cohorts with 100+ graduates. We've built operational playbooks that other Dutch AI safety groups are already using. And we've been reaching out to groups in Amsterdam, Utrecht, and other cities to help them build similar things.

At some point, it became clear: what we've built in Groningen is too useful to keep local. A national organization with a shared brand, shared infrastructure, and a single legal entity lets us do what we're already doing, but better. And it lets other cities do the same without having to start from scratch.

That's what SAIN is.

## **What changes?**

* **The name.** We are now **SAIN Groningen**, part of Safe AI Netherlands.  
* **Social media.** Our accounts will be rebranded to reflect the SAIN identity. The Substack became the national SAIN Substack.  
* **The website.** [safeainetherlands.org](https://safeainetherlands.org) is the new home. SAIN Groningen has its own section there. The old AISIG links will be redirected.  
* **Email.** New email addresses under the [safeainetherlands.org](http://safeainetherlands.org) domain are now established. Old emails to the [aisig.org](http://aisig.org) domain will still be redirected.

## **What doesn't change?**

* **Your team.** Same people, same leads, same roles.  
* **Your courses.** The AI Safety, Ethics, and Society course continues exactly as before.  
* **Your events.** Hackathons, AI Safety Chats, seminars, socials. All continuing.  
* **Your Research Hub projects.** Ongoing research is unaffected. In fact, the Research Hub is now national, which means more people, more supervisors, and more opportunities.  
* **Your discussion groups.** AI Governance and Technical AI Alignment. Both staying.  
* **Your community.** WhatsApp, Discord: same spaces, same people. The group names have been updated, that's it.  
* **Your autonomy.** SAIN Groningen runs its own programs, its own events, its own community. The national layer provides infrastructure and support, not micromanagement.

## **What gets better?**

Honestly, for Groningen specifically, the day-to-day doesn't change much. At least, for now. What changes immediately is the bigger picture:

* **Your Research Hub work now carries a national brand.** Publications and projects are backed by the authority of a national organization, not just a city initiative.  
* **More people in the pipeline.** Other chapters funnel people into the same Research Hub, the same events, the same community. The network gets bigger and richer.  
* **Proven playbooks go further.** The operational model we built together (the 4-team structure, the course curriculum, the facilitator guides) now helps other cities do the same. Your work created the blueprint.
