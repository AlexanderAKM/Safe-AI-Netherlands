# Safe AI Netherlands 

---

## In one paragraph

**Safe AI Netherlands (SAIN)** is a Dutch civil-society foundation (*Stichting Safe AI Netherlands*) that grew from the AI Safety Initiative Groningen into a national chapter network. We help people in the Netherlands understand the full range of risks from advanced AI, from technical failure modes to governance and societal impacts, and we turn that understanding into pathways for learning, community, and research. Local chapters (**Groningen, Amsterdam, Utrecht**) host courses based on established AI safety curricula (technical and governance tracks), weekly discussion groups, and public-facing events such as seminars, hackathons, and social meetups; nationally we run **SAIN Research Hub**, matching motivated contributors with supervised research projects aligned with frontier AI safety. We centralize brand, curricula, coordination, **fiscal sponsorship** for chapters, and funder-ready administration so chapters can focus on what truly matters instead of reinventing operations.

## What we do (concrete programmes)


| Area                        | What participants see                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Education**               | Multi-week “AI Safety, Ethics, and Society” course (Technical / Governance tracks), cohort-based, certificate on completion                                               |
| **Community**               | Mentored weekly discussion groups; city-level events including talks and hackathons (e.g. with partners such as Apart Research)                                           |
| **Research**                | SAIN Research Hub: structured projects, supervisor matching, documented expectations and norms                                                                            |
| **Outreach**                | Public narrative and awareness via events, chapters, LinkedIn company presence, Substack (**[Safe AI Netherlands on Substack](https://safeainetherlands.substack.com/)**) |
| **National infrastructure** | Shared legal entity, earmarked budgets for chapters under fiscal sponsorship oversight, playbook-style support for launching and running chapters                         |


Advisory Board
SAIN combines national coordination with chapter autonomy led by volunteer teams. Existing advisory voices on the organisation’s About page tilt toward technical AI safety and AI governance/policy. Building strength in media, strategic communications, financial sustainability, and nonprofit best practice is a necessary next step.
